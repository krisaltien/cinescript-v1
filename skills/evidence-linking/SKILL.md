---
name: evidence-linking
description: Link claims to short, exact source quotes for deterministic position resolution.
---

Attach evidence IDs to claims. Copy a short exact quote with its paragraph ID. The quote must uniquely occur in that paragraph. Reuse evidence IDs across claims where appropriate. The application calculates offsets; never calculate or emit them. If a passage crosses paragraphs, use multiple evidence entries. Mark unsupported claims unresolved.
