Identify explicitly present characters, names, descriptions and supporting paragraphs. Do not infer unnamed people as known characters. Extract only plot-relevant or relationship-relevant characters. Exclude people mentioned once as historical background, decorative context, incidental staff or service workers, passersby, or minor scene participants with no meaningful relationship. They may remain in evidence text without becoming character records.
